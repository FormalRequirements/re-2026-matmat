=== G.3 Expected Benefits

NOTE: New processes, or improvements to existing processes, made possible by the project’s results (see {Handbook}).

WARNING: This chapter should not be empty (following the Minimum Requirements Outcome Principle, p.27 of the {handbook}).

The 404 Association website project is expected to deliver a set of strategic benefits that directly support the visibility, cohesion, and operational efficiency of the student community. These benefits justify the investment and provide a clear rationale for the system’s development and deployment.

By clearly identifying and documenting these expected benefits, the project maintains alignment with its original goals, especially as priorities shift or project direction is challenged. The benefits also serve to constrain the scope and protect against unnecessary additions ("creeping featurism") that may dilute its focus.

The website enhances organizational resilience through improved communication, talent promotion, and streamlined activity management.
