=== G.3 Expected Benefits

The 404 Association website project is expected to deliver strategic benefits that justify the investment.

[[G3-1]]
^G.3.{counter:g3}^ **Permanent Visibility**::
The system shall mitigate the association's lack of digital presence by providing an official and permanent online platform accessible 24/7.

[[G3-2]]
^G.3.{counter:g3}^ **Member Professionalization**::
The system shall contribute to the professionalization of its members by providing a structured showcase for their technical projects.

[[G3-3]]
^G.3.{counter:g3}^ **Administrative Autonomy**::
The system shall streamline the board's daily operations by enabling administrators to manage and update content without external technical expertise.
