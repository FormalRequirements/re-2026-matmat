=== G.1 Context and overall objectives

The 404 Association brings together students in the Mathematics and Computer Science Applied to Human and Social Sciences (MIASHS) fields.
The "404 Web Project" addresses the need for a centralized information hub to showcase student achievements and provide a dynamic management interface.

[[G1-1]]
^G.1.{counter:g1}^ **Showcase Visibility**::
The system shall provide a public presentation of the 404 Association’s missions, values, and organization to increase its institutional visibility.

[[G1-2]]
^G.1.{counter:g1}^ **Student Promotion**::
The system shall provide a dedicated space for showcasing student projects and academic paths to help industrial and academic partners identify potential candidates.

[[G1-3]]
^G.1.{counter:g1}^ **Dynamic Management**::
The system shall allow an authenticated user (Administrator) to add, modify, or delete activities and events autonomously through a dedicated management interface.

[[G1-4]]
^G.1.{counter:g1}^ **Responsive Accessibility**::
The system shall be designed to provide a smooth and consistent user experience across all devices (desktops, tablets, and smartphones) using a responsive design approach.

[[G1-5]]
^G.1.{counter:g1}^ **Technical Reliability**::
The system shall be developed using modern technologies (React/TypeScript, Firebase) to ensure long-term maintainability.
