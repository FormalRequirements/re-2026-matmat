=== G.1 Context and overall objectives

NOTE: High-level view of the project: organizational context (404 Association) and reason for building the system.

WARNING: This chapter defines the core foundation for the MIASHS student association web platform.

The 404 Association brings together students in the Mathematics and Computer Science Applied to Human and Social Sciences (MIASHS) fields, from Undergraduate to Master's levels. It was established with a vision to foster learning, innovation, and mutual aid through collaborative projects and events. Currently, the lack of an official web platform limits the visibility of members' skills and the promotion of activities to external partners. The "404 Web Project" addresses the need for a centralized information hub to showcase student achievements and provide a dynamic management interface for association life.

The overall objectives of the system are to ensure a professional digital storefront, facilitate connections between students and recruiters, and allow for simplified content administration (activities and events) without requiring direct modifications to the source code.
