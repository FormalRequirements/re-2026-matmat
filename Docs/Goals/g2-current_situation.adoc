=== G.2 Current Situation

NOTE: Current state of processes to be addressed by the project and the resulting system (see {Handbook}).

This chapter provides a structured overview of the current situation regarding the digital communication, member management, and external visibility of the 404 Association. Each relevant aspect is assessed on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 indicates major insufficiency and 5 indicates full satisfaction. This analysis motivates the need for the new web platform.

==== Evaluation of current practices

[cols="3,1,3",options="header"] |=== | Category | Score (1–5) | Comment

| Official digital presence | 1 | The association has no centralized website; visibility relies solely on ephemeral social media posts. | Student project visibility | 2 | Academic projects are shared informally on private channels (Discord), remaining invisible to recruiters. | Event information access | 2 | Event details are often lost in messaging threads, leading to confusion among students. | Content update autonomy | 1 | Updating any public information currently requires technical intervention or manual reposting. | Institutional branding | 2 | The association lacks a consistent professional image for interacting with university partners. | Resource availability | 3 | Study materials and guides exist but are difficult for new members (L1) to locate and access. | Recruitment contact channel | 1 | No dedicated, professional channel exists for companies to contact the association regarding internships. |===

==== Summary of weaknesses

Several limitations emerge from the evaluation above:

//---- Weakness [[g2-credibility_gap]] ^G.2.1.{counter:g21}^ The absence of an official website creates a credibility gap when approaching external partners, sponsors, and laboratories. //----

//---- Weakness [[g2-information_fragmentation]] ^G.2.1.{counter:g21}^ The fragmentation of critical information across multiple social networks leads to lower student engagement and missed opportunities. //----

//---- Weakness [[g2-hidden_talent]] ^G.2.1.{counter:g21}^ The lack of a public portfolio platform prevents the effective promotion of students' technical skills to potential employers. //----
