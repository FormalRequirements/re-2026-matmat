=== G.2 Current Situation

This chapter provides a structured overview of the current situation regarding digital communication. Each aspect is assessed on a scale from 1 (insufficient) to 5 (satisfactory).

.Evaluation of current practices
[cols="3,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Category | Score | Comment

| **Official digital presence**
| 1
| The association has no centralized website; visibility relies solely on ephemeral social media posts.

| **Student project visibility**
| 2
| Academic projects are shared informally on private channels (Discord), remaining invisible to recruiters.

| **Event information access**
| 2
| Event details are often lost in messaging threads, leading to confusion among students.

| **Content update autonomy**
| 1
| Updating any public information currently requires technical intervention or manual reposting.
|===

==== Summary of weaknesses

[[G2-1]]
^G.2.{counter:g2}^ **Credibility Gap**::
The absence of an official website creates a credibility gap when approaching external partners, sponsors, and laboratories.

[[G2-2]]
^G.2.{counter:g2}^ **Information Fragmentation**::
The fragmentation of critical information across multiple social networks leads to lower student engagement and missed opportunities.

[[G2-3]]
^G.2.{counter:g2}^ **Hidden Talent**::
The lack of a public portfolio platform prevents the effective promotion of students' technical skills to potential employers.
