=== G.5 High-level usage scenarios

[cite_start]This chapter describes fundamental usage scenarios from the point of view of stakeholders[cite: 72].

[[G5-1]]
^G.5.{counter:g5}^ **Scenario: Student engages with activities**::
. A new student (L1) wants to get involved but does not know upcoming dates.
. The student accesses the public homepage and navigates to the "Events" section.
. They view a chronological list of upcoming workshops and parties.
. They click on a specific event to read the full description and find the location.
. **Result:** The student is informed and can attend without needing to ask via private messaging.
