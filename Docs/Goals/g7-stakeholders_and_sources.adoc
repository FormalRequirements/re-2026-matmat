=== G.7 Stakeholders and requirements sources

This chapter identifies the stakeholders and sources contributing to the requirements.

[[G7-1]]
^G.7.{counter:g7}^ **Competitor Benchmarking**::
Analysis of websites from other student associations (BDE, other universities) was performed to identify best practices.

[[G7-2]]
^G.7.{counter:g7}^ **Legal Standards (GDPR)**::
The project must comply with data protection regulations regarding the collection of personal info via contact forms.

[[G7-3]]
^G.7.{counter:g7}^ **Design Guidelines**::
The specific visual identity (colors, fonts) provided by the Association's communication team serves as a constraint for the UI.
