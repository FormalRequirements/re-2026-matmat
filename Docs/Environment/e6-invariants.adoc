=== E.6 Invariants

NOTE: Properties of the environment that are always true, regardless of the system's behavior.

For this web application project, the environmental invariants (such as "A user cannot be in two places at once" or "Time moves forward") are considered trivial or implied.

Specific constraints regarding the validity of data are treated as **Business Rules** in section **E.3 Constraints** or as **Assumptions** in section **E.4**. Consequently, this section acts as a placeholder to satisfy the PEGS structure but contains no additional specific invariants.
