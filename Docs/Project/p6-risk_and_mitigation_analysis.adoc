=== P.6 Risk and Mitigation Analysis

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation (Solution)

| **Knowledge Loss**
| High. The association board changes every year; the site risks becoming unmaintainable.
| Exhaustive technical documentation and commented code. Choice of standard technologies (React).

| **Third-Party Dependency**
| Medium. Firebase or Vercel change their free tier plans.
| Modular architecture allowing a switch of backend providers if necessary. Quota monitoring.

| **Development Delays**
| High. Significant academic workload in parallel.
| Strict prioritization via the MoSCoW method (functionalities marked as "MUST" are priority).
|===
